Is this a government survey?

No. Indian Taxpayer Voice is an independent initiative and this is not an official government survey.

Will taking part get me insurance or any benefit?

No. We do not provide insurance or any financial product, and taking part does not entitle you to any benefit.

Is the survey anonymous?

We do not ask for your name, PAN, Aadhaar or contact details in the survey. If you choose to receive updates, your email address is stored separately from your survey answers. Please avoid writing identifying details in free-text answers.

Do the results represent all taxpayers?

No. They describe the people who chose to respond. See the methodology.
