How it works

From your answers to public evidence

1

You answer a short survey

Your state or union territory, your broad sector of work, and the concerns that matter most to you. No identity documents or financial details.

2

We publish only totals

Answers are combined. Small groups are hidden so that no one can be singled out, and individual responses are never published.

3

Evidence anyone can check

Every figure comes with its method, its limits and the date it was last updated, so the evidence can be scrutinised openly.
