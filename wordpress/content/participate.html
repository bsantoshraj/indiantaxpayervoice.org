The survey takes about five minutes and has twelve short questions. It asks which state or union territory you live in, your broad employment sector, and which concerns matter most to you. Questions about income range, gender and age are optional, and every one of them has a “Prefer not to say” option.

Before you start

  • Participation is voluntary and you can stop at any time.
  • This is not an official government survey.
  • Taking part does not provide any financial, insurance or other benefit.
  • We do not ask for your PAN, Aadhaar, tax returns, exact salary, bank details, employer name, medical records or address.
  • Please do not include personal details in the optional comments box.
