Purpose

To understand the concerns reported by people who choose to take part, broken down by state or union territory and broad employment sector.

Questionnaire version

v1-draft (not yet launched). Survey dates will be recorded here when the survey opens.

Who can take part

Anyone who chooses to respond. Recruitment channels will be listed here as they are used.

Valid responses

  • Only completed responses with consent are counted.
  • Responses with state, union territory or sector codes outside the questionnaire options are excluded.
  • Repeated submissions of the same response record (for example, after a network retry) are counted once.
  • Incomplete responses are not included in published figures.

Limitations

This is a voluntary online survey. People who choose to respond may differ from the wider population, for example in internet access, language, interest in the topic, or how they heard about the survey. Results describe respondents only and are not statistically representative of Indian taxpayers, however many people take part.

How figures are calculated

  • Percentages use valid responses as the denominator unless stated otherwise.
  • Respondents may select more than one concern, so concern percentages can add up to more than 100%.
  • In the industry-by-concern table, percentages are the share of that sector’s respondents.
  • Groups with fewer than 20 responses are not shown. Some larger groups are also hidden so that a small group cannot be worked out by subtraction.
  • States and union territories with no responses are shown as “No responses”, which is different from “Not shown”.
  • State and union territory are as reported by respondents. We do not infer location from IP addresses.

Refresh schedule

Published statistics are recalculated on a schedule and show the time of the last refresh.
