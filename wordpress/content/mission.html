Mission

To gather and publish transparent evidence about the concerns of Indian taxpayers, so that public discussion of job-loss and health-related financial protection is informed by what people actually report.

Principles

  • Non-partisan: we do not support or oppose any political party or candidate.
  • Evidence-based: we publish our methods and limitations alongside our findings.
  • Transparent: we explain how every published figure is calculated.
  • Privacy-conscious: we collect only what the research needs and never publish individual responses.
  • Honest about scope: we do not provide insurance, financial products or benefits.
