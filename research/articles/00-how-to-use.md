# Research articles for sharing (drafts)

Oct 24, 2026 · @santosh

Twelve short, sourced articles on questions Indian taxpayers search for, each with ready-to-post versions for LinkedIn, X and WhatsApp that point to the survey. Every figure links to the page it came from; check the numbers once more before posting, because official data is revised.

## How to use these drafts

Post one article every two or three days; each has a long version for LinkedIn and short versions for X and WhatsApp. Use the tracked link for the channel, so analytics can show which channel brings people who finish the survey.

Before posting each one:

- [ ] Open every source link and check the figure is still current (budgets and surveys are revised).
- [ ] Keep the wording non-partisan: no party, politician or scheme praised or attacked.
- [ ] Do not quote survey results until the statistics page shows them; never present results as representative of all taxpayers.
- [ ] For X, put the link in a reply to your own post; posts with links in them often reach fewer people.
