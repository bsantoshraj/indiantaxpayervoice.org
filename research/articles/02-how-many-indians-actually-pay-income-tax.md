# 2. How many Indians actually pay income tax?

**Far fewer people pay income tax than file a return, and far fewer file than most people think.**

**Who files.** About 8.09 crore income tax returns (8,09,03,315) were filed in 2023-24. That is about 6.68% of the population, according to Minister of State for Finance Pankaj Chaudhary's written reply in the Rajya Sabha in December 2024 ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/only-6-68-citizens-filed-i-t-returns-in-2023-24)). The same reply said 4.9 crore individuals reported zero taxable income in AY 2023-24.

**Filing is not paying.** Many returns show no tax due, mainly because of exemptions and the rebate. In July 2023 the Finance Minister told the Lok Sabha that 5.16 crore of the 7.40 crore returns for 2022-23 had zero tax liability, as did 5.05 crore of 6.94 crore for 2021-22 ([Deccan Herald, PTI](https://www.deccanherald.com/business/74-cr-itrs-filed-in-fy23-over-516-cr-declared-zero-tax-liability-says-fm-nirmala-sitharaman-1240245)). A later Lok Sabha reply, as reported by a tax-software publisher, put the zero-liability share at about two in three returns: 5.58 crore of 8.39 crore in 2024-25, up to 31 December ([SAG Infotech](https://blog.saginfotech.com/mos-pankaj-chaudhary-66-5-percent-income-taxpayers-zero-tax-liability-fy-2024-25)).

**Still growing.** More than 6 crore returns had been filed for AY 2025-26 by 13 September 2025, the Income Tax Department said ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/over-6-crore-income-tax-returns-filed-so-far-i-t-dept)).

**Why it matters.** The people who do pay direct tax are a small group, and most of them are salaried, with tax deducted at source. What do they think they get back? That is what our survey asks.
